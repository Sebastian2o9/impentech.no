Privacy policy

How personal data is handled on this website and in Atlas (web and iPhone).

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

This website and enquiries to us: Impen Tech AS (org. no. 937 984 626), Meåsvegen 9, 3840 Seljord, Norway, is the controller.

Atlas used through your employer: Atlas is a work tool that your employer gives you access to. Your employer is then the controller for the use of Atlas, and Impen Tech processes the data on your employer's behalf under an agreement with your employer (a data processing agreement). You can direct questions about your rights to your employer or to us at post@impentech.onmicrosoft.com.

2. This website

  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We do not ourselves collect personal data through this website, and it has no forms.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub). As with any website, the host may process technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs in order to deliver and secure the site. We do not use such information for analytics or marketing.
  • If you send us an email, we only use the information in it to reply and follow up on your enquiry. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

3. Atlas (web and iPhone)

When you use Atlas, the following information is processed:

Sign-in

You sign in with the Microsoft account provided by your employer (Microsoft Entra ID). The service receives the account information needed to give you access, such as your name, email address and a user ID. We never receive your password.

Conversations and projects

The questions you write to Atlas, the answers and the projects you create in Atlas Studio are stored so that the service works — for example so that a conversation can continue and so that you can reopen a project on another device. Conversations may also be reviewed, manually and automatically, to check and improve the quality of the answers. Please do not enter unnecessary personal information about customers in conversations.

Camera and images

The camera is only used when you open the scanner or take a picture yourself: to read barcodes, and to take a picture of a product that is sent to the service to identify the product. Images you choose from your photo library for a project are stored with faces masked and without location data. Room scanning with LiDAR stays on the phone. You can turn off access to the camera and photos at any time in your iPhone settings.

Technical information

To operate and secure the service, technical information such as timestamps, error messages and the app version in use is recorded.

Providers

Atlas runs on Microsoft Azure, and sign-in goes through Microsoft Entra ID. These providers only process information in order to deliver the service. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising. If you would like to know where the data is stored, just ask us.

4. Purpose

Data in Atlas is processed to provide the service, give support, keep the service secure and stable, and check and improve the quality of the answers. When Atlas is used through your employer, your employer as the controller determines the legal basis for the processing.

5. How long we keep data

  • Operational logs that contain conversation text: deleted after 90 days.
  • Quality assessments of answers: deleted after 180 days.
  • Images: deleted after 90 days, unless an image has to be kept longer to fix a fault.
  • Projects and conversation history: kept for as long as the account is active in the service, or until your employer or you ask for deletion.

Data may be kept longer where the law requires it.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access the information about you, and to request rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability or to object to the processing. Contact your employer or send an email to post@impentech.onmicrosoft.com. If you believe data is processed in breach of the rules, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.no).

7. Children

Atlas is a work tool and is not intended for children.

8. Changes

We update this policy when the service changes. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

9. Contact
