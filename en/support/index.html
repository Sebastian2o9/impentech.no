Frequently asked questions

Questions and answers

What is Atlas?

Atlas is a digital assistant for staff in building supply retail. It answers questions about products and projects, finds products in the catalogue, shows product passports and lets you plan rooms in Atlas Studio.

How do I sign in?

You sign in with the Microsoft account provided by your employer. There is no separate registration — access is granted through your employer.

I cannot sign in. What should I do?

First check that you are using your work account and that you are online. If that does not help, contact the IT administrator at your employer or send us an email with the time and any error message.

Where does the product data come from?

Products, dimensions, packs and documents come from NOBB, the Norwegian building industry's product database. Atlas shows the date of the catalogue data.

I cannot find a product. Why?

Try searching by NOBB number, barcode or the supplier's article number — an exact code gives the exact product. If the code returns nothing, the product is not in our catalogue data or may have been discontinued. Atlas then tells you so instead of suggesting a different product.

Why does the colour say “approximate”?

Atlas only shows colours that the product itself states with a colour code, such as NCS or RAL. A screen cannot reproduce a colour exactly, so the colour is always marked as approximate. Use a physical colour sample before you decide.

What does “Illustration” mean in Atlas Studio?

When we do not have a faithful rendering of a product, Studio shows a neutral surface or shape labelled “Illustration”. The product is still included in the materials list with the correct data.

Can I rely on the quantities?

Quantities are calculated from the measurements you enter and rounded up to whole packs, and the calculation is shown. Always check the measurements, and have a qualified professional confirm solutions involving load-bearing structures, wet rooms, fire safety, electrics and other critical matters.

How does the app use the camera?

The camera is only used when you open the scanner or take a picture yourself: to read barcodes, and to take a picture of a product that is sent to the service to identify the product. Images you choose from your photo library for a project are stored with faces masked and without location data, and images are deleted after 90 days. Room scanning with LiDAR stays on the phone. You can turn off access to the camera and photos at any time in your iPhone settings.

How do I delete my data?

Send an email to post@impentech.onmicrosoft.com from your work address and we will help you. Read more in the privacy policy.

Is the iPhone app available?

The iPhone app is in development and coming to the App Store. It requires iOS 17 or later. Until then you can use Atlas in the browser.

Which devices are supported?

Atlas works in up-to-date versions of common browsers on computers, tablets and phones. The iPhone app requires iOS 17 or later.

Atlas is a tool. Its answers are meant as support, not as the only basis for decisions.
