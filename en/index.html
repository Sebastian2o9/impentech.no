How it works

From the customer's question to a finished shopping list

  1. The customer explains

    Staff type in the question or look up the product the customer has brought.

  2. Atlas understands the job

    Atlas reads what needs doing and where, and finds the products in the catalogue.

  3. Quantities and packs

    Quantities are calculated from the customer's measurements and rounded up to whole packs.

  4. Show the result

    In Atlas Studio the customer sees the room in 2D, 3D and as a photorealistic image.

Product passport

The answer to “what exactly is this product?”

Type or scan a code and Atlas opens the product passport for exactly that product: supplier, product group, packs with barcodes, properties and the documents that belong to it.

  • An exact code gives the exact product, and an unknown code says so honestly
  • Operation and maintenance documentation (FDV), safety data sheets and installation instructions where available
  • Every property shows where the information comes from

Quantities

The calculation is shown, not just the answer

The materials list shows how each quantity was calculated: area, number of coats, coverage per litre and what was subtracted for doors and windows. Quantities are rounded up to whole packs, and lines that cannot be ordered yet are clearly marked.

Honesty

Atlas tells you when it does not know

An in-store assistant has to be trustworthy. That is why Atlas shows what is documented, what is approximate and what is only an illustration.

approximate

Colours from a documented code

A colour is only shown when the product itself states a colour code (for example NCS or RAL). It is always marked as approximate.

Illustration

Illustrations are labelled

When there is no faithful rendering of a product, Studio shows a neutral surface labelled “Illustration” — never an image pretending to be the product.

professional

A tool, not the final word

Atlas supports staff. Load-bearing work, wet rooms, fire safety, electrics and other critical matters must always be confirmed by a qualified professional.
