How you work in Studio

Four steps from empty room to finished image

  1. Measure the room

    Enter width, length and height. Measurements always come from you, never from a product name.

  2. Choose products

    Pick a wall, floor or ceiling and search the catalogue by name or code. Studio checks that the product fits the surface.

  3. Doors, windows, furniture

    Add doors and windows to the walls and place furniture using the product's own dimensions.

  4. List and image

    Get a materials list with quantities and packs, and create a photorealistic image of the room.

The workspace

See the room in 3D, from above in 2D, or as a photo

The room is in the middle and the surfaces with their measurements on the right. Everything is saved as versions, so you can undo, save alternatives and compare.

Materials list

Quantities from the room — with the working shown

Studio works out how much of each product is needed from the room's surfaces. Doors and windows are subtracted from the wall area, and quantities are rounded up to whole packs.

  • Quantity, pack and status on every line
  • “Unresolved” when a product needs choosing
  • Print or save as PDF

Photorealistic image

From 3D view to image in one click

“Create image” turns the room into a photorealistic image from the same camera angle. Doors and windows have the same dimensions in the image as in the 3D view, and the image can be downloaded and given to the customer.

Living room · 4.20 × 5.00 × 2.50 m. Two paints, one door and two windows from the catalogue. No floor has been chosen in this example.

  • Colour only from a documented code
  • Marked “approximate”
  • Illustrations are labelled as such
  • The image lists the products it is based on

Everywhere

From the counter PC to the phone in the warehouse

Studio works in the browser on computers, tablets and phones, and the iPhone app is in development. Projects are saved to your account so you can open them wherever you are.

  • Saved versions and alternatives you can compare
  • The date of the product data is always shown
