About us

Impen Tech AS

A Norwegian technology company in Seljord, Telemark. We build Atlas, a digital assistant for staff in building supply stores.

We build tools that make it easier to find the right product, calculate the right quantity and show the customer the result — without staff having to know the entire catalogue by heart.

Why we build Atlas

In a building supply store, staff field questions about everything from a single screw to a whole bathroom. The range is enormous, and the customer wants an answer that is correct. Atlas brings the catalogue, the calculations and the view of the room together in one place.

How we think

Data before guesswork

Products, dimensions, packs and documents come from catalogue data — NOBB, the building industry's shared product database — not from a language model's memory.

Honest about what we do not know

When information is missing, Atlas says so. Colours are marked as approximate, illustrations are labelled as illustrations, and critical matters are referred to a qualified professional.

Made for the shop floor

Atlas is built for short questions, busy days and screens of every size — from the PC at the counter to the phone in your pocket.

Meet Atlas

The carpenter is the face of Atlas in the app. He shows that the assistant is working — and reminds us that it is skilled people who build.
