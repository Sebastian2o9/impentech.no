About us

Seljord, Norway

Tools for the people in the store.

Impen Tech AS is a Norwegian technology company with its registered address in Seljord, Telemark. We build Atlas, a digital assistant for staff in building supply stores, and Atlas Studio, where the customer sees the room before the first coat.

We build tools that make it easier to find the right product, work out the right quantity and show the customer the result — without staff having to know the entire catalogue by heart.

Why we build Atlas

In a building supply store, staff field questions about everything from a single screw to a whole bathroom. The range is enormous, and the customer wants an answer that is right. Atlas brings the catalogue, the calculations and the picture of the room together in one place — and is honest about what it does not know.

How we think

Three principles we stick to.

  • 01

    Data before guesswork

    Products, dimensions, packs and documents come from catalogue data — NOBB, the building industry’s shared product database — not from guesswork.

  • 02

    Honest about what we do not know

    When information is missing, Atlas says so. Colours are marked as approximate, illustrations are labelled as illustrations, and critical matters are referred to a qualified professional.

  • 03

    Made for the shop floor

    Atlas is built for short questions, busy days and screens of every size — from the PC at the counter to the phone in your pocket.
