Atlas — the assistant for the shop floor

What do I need, how much do I need, and what exactly is this product? Atlas helps building supply staff with what customers actually ask.

Conversation

Understands the project — not just the words

Staff type the customer's question the way it is asked. Atlas works out what needs doing, where, and which products belong to the job, and answers with products from the catalogue and quantities the customer can shop by.

  • Suggests the products the job needs — including the easily forgotten ones
  • Measurements and quantities are only taken from what the customer stated
  • Follow-up questions build on the same conversation
  • Products come from a local copy of the catalogue, so answers are fast and consistent

Example: “Painting the living room, 4 × 5 metres, 2.4 m ceiling height”

Atlas understands it is interior walls, suggests paint and primer, and works out litres and the number of tins from the wall area the customer gave.

Quantities you can check

The calculation is shown: area, coats, coverage and deductions. If something is unclear, Atlas asks rather than guesses.

Search and product passport

More than 760,000 building products, four ways in

The catalogue is based on NOBB, the Norwegian building industry's product database. Search by name when the customer describes a product, or use a code when you have one in front of you.

NAMEchipboard flooring 22 mm NOBB2231 0718 GTIN7392951100094 SUPPLIERA12220A10009
  • An exact code gives the exact product — never a “nearest” one
  • The product passport (one page per product) gathers dimensions, variants, packs and barcodes
  • Documentation: operation and maintenance documentation (FDV), safety data sheets and installation instructions
  • Every detail has a source, and the date of the catalogue data is shown

Phone

Atlas on the phone

Atlas works in the phone's browser today. The iPhone app is in development and coming to the App Store. It uses the same service, so conversations, product passports and Studio projects stay connected.

  • In the iPhone app: scan the barcode on the shelf or package and get the right product passport
  • Continue a Studio project that was started on the in-store computer
  • Sign in with the work account from your employer

The camera is only used when you open the scanner or take a picture yourself. Read more in the privacy policy.

Who it is for

Made for the people in the store

Staff and temps

New in the job or in an unfamiliar department? Atlas gives the confidence to answer the customer correctly — and to show why.

Customers with a project

The customer leaves with a list of products and quantities, and an image of how the room could look.

Stores and chains

Staff sign in with their employer's Microsoft account — no extra passwords to manage.

Atlas is a tool. Its answers are meant as support, and product choices and solutions involving load-bearing structures, wet rooms, fire safety, electrics and other critical matters must be confirmed by a qualified professional.
