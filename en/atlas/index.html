The product — Atlas

Web-based work tool

The assistant for the shop floor.

What do I need, how much do I need — and what exactly is this product? Atlas helps building supply staff with what customers actually ask.

Request a demoAtlas Studio

01 — Conversation

Ask the way the customer asks — Atlas does the rest.

Staff type the customer’s question in their own words. Atlas finds the job, the place and the measurements, and answers with the products the job needs.

Atlas suggests the whole job — including the easily forgotten parts. Measurements and quantities come only from the customer’s own words, follow-up questions build on the same conversation, and products, sizes and packs come from the catalogue — not from guesswork.

02 — Search

More than 760,000 products, four ways in.

The catalogue is based on NOBB, the Norwegian building industry’s product database. Search by name when the customer describes a product, or use a code when you have one in front of you.

  • An exact code gives the exact product — never a “nearest” one
  • If a code finds nothing, Atlas says so instead of suggesting something else
  • The date of the catalogue data is always shown

03 — Product passport

One page per product, with the source stated.

When the customer is holding a pack, its code opens the product passport for exactly that product. It gathers what staff need to answer — and shows the date of the catalogue data.

1Overview
Supplier, product group, price unit, stocked according to NOBB and the supplier’s article number.
2Packs
Every pack with its barcode (GTIN), pack type and contents.
3Properties
The values the product is registered with — and where each piece of information comes from.
4Documents
FDV (operation and maintenance), safety data sheets and installation instructions where available, marked when they apply to this exact product.
5Variants
Other sizes and versions of the same product.

04 — Quantities

The working is shown — not just the answer.

Area, number of coats and coverage, minus doors and windows. The answer is rounded up to whole packs, so the customer gets something that can actually be bought.

See the full working for the living room
  • Walls30 m² × 2 coats ÷ 8 m²/l=7.5 l→1 × 10 l
  • Accent wall11 m² × 2 coats ÷ 8 m²/l=2.7 l→6 × 0.5 l
  • Floor21 m² × 2 coats ÷ 8 m²/l=5.2 l→1 × 10 l
  • Primer29.9 m² ÷ 10 m²/l=3.0 l→unresolved
  • Oak parquet · alternative21.0 m² + 10% cutting waste=23.1 m²→9 × 2.72 m²

The parquet line is calculated for the same room, saved as an alternative with oak parquet (NOBB 4660 6072, packs of 2.72 m²) instead of floor paint. Floor coverings get 10% for cutting waste, and the underlay stays unresolved until one is chosen.

05 — Everywhere

At the counter, in the warehouse — in the browser.

Atlas is web-based and works in up-to-date browsers on computers, tablets and phones. There is nothing to install: staff sign in with the work account from their employer.

  • The same Atlas on the counter screen and on the phone in the warehouse
  • Studio projects are saved to the account and can be opened wherever you are
  • Sign-in with the employer’s Microsoft account — no extra passwords

06 — Who it is for

Made for the people in the store.

  • Staff

    Staff and temps

    New in the job or in an unfamiliar department? Atlas gives the confidence to answer the customer correctly — and to show why.

  • Customers

    Customers with a project

    The customer leaves with a list of products and quantities, and an image of how the room could look.

  • Store

    Stores and chains

    Access is granted through the employer, and staff sign in with the work account they already have.

Atlas is a tool. Its answers are meant as support, and product choices and solutions involving load-bearing structures, wet rooms, fire safety, electrics and other critical matters must be confirmed by a qualified professional.
